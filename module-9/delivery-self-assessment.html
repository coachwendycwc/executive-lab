Delivery Self-Assessment

A great story told apologetically loses. Score how you actually tell yours, then pick two things to sharpen.

How to use this worksheet

Say your Signature Story out loud first, ideally recorded. Then rate each dimension from 1 (not yet) to 5 (consistently true). There are no wrong scores; this is a baseline to work from. For additional accountability, use this activity with a partner or mentor who can give you feedback on your delivery. Answers auto-save as you go.
