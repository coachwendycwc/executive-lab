## SLIDE-BY-SLIDE
1. **TITLE (dark/gradient)** — "Executive Storytelling: Master Your Narrative" · Module 9 — September · lead on owning the story your work tells · meta: 12-Month Program · Coaching Women of Color.
2. **WHY THIS MODULE** — The work doesn't speak for itself; the story you tell about it is what travels into the rooms where you're discussed. Coming off summer, turn your wins into narrative. (callout, no stats)
3. **WHAT YOU'LL GAIN** — 4 objectives, each tied to a worksheet: (1) master 3 story structures; (2) convert wins into stories; (3) craft a 2-min Signature Story; (4) sharpen delivery.
4. **THE CORE IDEA** — From doing the work to owning the telling. A win no one hears about doesn't travel. Your narrative is an asset you build on purpose.
5. **THE KEY DISTINCTION** — February = STRATEGY (Who / When / Where) vs. September = TECHNIQUE (How to say it). Feb chose the rooms; Sep gives you the words. (equation / two-card)
6. **WHY STORY WORKS** — People remember and decide on stories, not bullet points. Structure carries facts into memory and credibility. (framework, no stats)
7. **QUOTE / REFLECTION (quote slide)** — program-voice line on narrative.
8. **THE STRUCTURES: STAR · CAR · CARL** — three cards. STAR (Situation, Task, Action, Result); CAR (Challenge, Action, Result); CARL (Challenge, Action, Result, Learning). When to use each.
9. **THE SIGNATURE STORY** — what it is: a 2-minute story that captures who you are and the value you bring; your default answer to "tell me about yourself" / "walk me through your impact."
10. **ACTIVITY — Worksheet 1: Story Structure Templates (STAR, CAR, CARL).** Structure one real win in each frame. 12–15 min.
11. **SETUP — You already have the material.** Your wins from the year (and the summer's reflection) are raw material; now convert them.
12. **ACTIVITY — Worksheet 2: Wins-to-Stories Converter.** Turn 3–5 wins into structured stories. 12–15 min.
13. **BUILD A PORTFOLIO** — Different rooms need different stories: interviews, performance reviews, leading teams. Same win, re-cut for the audience.
14. **A TOOL — The Signature Story shape** — Hook → Stakes → Action (your moves) → Result → Meaning (what it says about you). (equation)
15. **ACTIVITY — Worksheet 3: Signature Story Worksheet.** Draft the polished 2-min story. 12–15 min.
16. **WOC FOCUS** — Owning your narrative without minimizing accomplishments or centering others; claiming credit authentically. (two-column: Realities / Moves)
17. **DELIVERY** — How you tell it is half of it: ownership language ("I led," not "we kind of"), pacing, presence, landing the result. (cards)
18. **ACTIVITY — Worksheet 4: Delivery Self-Assessment Checklist.** Score your delivery, pick two things to sharpen. 8–10 min.
19. **THE 14-DAY SPRINT** — 3 actions: tell your Signature Story out loud to one person; convert two more wins; use one story in a real review or meeting.
20. **QUOTE / REFLECTION (dark)** — program-voice close line.
21. **KEY TAKEAWAYS** — 5 bullets, each tied to a worksheet / core idea.
22. **HOMEWORK & CLOSE (dark)** — homework (4 items incl. all four worksheets + community post) + **Builds into October:** a strong narrative is the foundation for leading others; October turns your voice outward — storytelling to build coalition and lead inclusively. (No references list — no stats cited.)
