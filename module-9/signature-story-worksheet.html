Signature Story Worksheet

Your 2-minute story: the one that captures who you are and the value you bring. Build it here, section by section.

How to use this worksheet

Draft each beat below, then stitch them into one story you could tell out loud in about two minutes. Aim for specific over impressive. Answers auto-save as you type.

Hook+ Stakes+ Your Action+ Result+ Meaning
1
Hook
The first line that makes someone lean in. Open in the middle of the action, not with a resume.
2
Stakes
Why it mattered. What was at risk if it went wrong?
3
Your Action
The specific moves you made. First person: “I decided,” “I built,” “I led.”
4
Result
The outcome, stated plainly. A number lands harder than an adjective.
5
Meaning
The beat most people skip. What does this story say about who you are and how you lead?
6
Put It Together
Stitch the five beats into one story, in your own voice. Read it out loud and time it: aim for about two minutes.
7
Tighten It
Read it aloud once more and check each box honestly.
