How to use this worksheet

Pick one real win. Run it through each of the three structures below. The point is to feel how the frame changes what you emphasize, so you can choose the right one for each room. Answers auto-save as you type.

The Win You'll Work With

STAR
Situation, Task, Action, Result
The full frame. Best for interviews and reviews where context matters.
CAR
Challenge, Action, Result
The tight frame. Best when time is short and the result should land fast.
CARL
Challenge, Action, Result, Learning
Adds reflection. Best for growth conversations and leading teams.
