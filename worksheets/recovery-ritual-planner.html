Recovery Ritual Planner

Rest that restores is designed, not stumbled into. Build rituals at three scales, so recovery happens on purpose, not only once you've crashed.

How to use this worksheet

Design recovery at three scales: micro (small resets inside a day), meso (a weekly recharge), and macro (seasonal, deeper renewal). For each, name the ritual, its cue, how long it takes, and how you'll protect it. Then set the boundaries and non-negotiables that keep it real. Answers auto-save as you type.

1
Micro · daily
Small Resets Inside the Day
Two to five minutes that interrupt the depletion before it compounds: a breath between meetings, a step outside, a hand on your own shoulder.
Cue = the moment that triggers it (e.g., "after every meeting," "when my jaw tightens"). Rituals with a cue happen; rituals without one get skipped.
