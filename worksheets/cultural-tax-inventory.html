How to use this worksheet

This is a structural inventory, not a personal audit. The "cultural tax" is imposed by systems and environments. Carrying it is not a flaw in you. Rate the toll each form takes, sort what's chosen from what's imposed, then decide what to renegotiate. Answers auto-save as you type.

Cultural tax

The extra, mostly invisible labor women of color are expected to carry at work, on top of the actual job. Being the "only" and the representative, the unpaid diversity work, code-switching, educating others about bias, and holding others' comfort. It is real work. It costs real energy. And it is usually neither counted nor compensated.

Educating others about bias

Emotional support for other marginalized colleagues

Being the safe person others come to: meaningful, and also a real draw on you.

Rarely Heavy toll
Managing others' comfort

Softening yourself so others don't feel threatened: monitoring tone, smiling through, making it easy for everyone else.
