Energy Audit

Energy is not the same as time. You can have a light calendar and still be depleted. Find out where yours actually goes.

Part of the Energy Reset & Cultural Tax Workbook · 1 of 3

How to use this worksheet

Think back over the past two weeks. Rate how each area of your life has left you feeling: drained on the low end, energized on the high end. Then map what drains you and map what restores you. There are no good or bad scores; this is a snapshot to work from. Answers auto-save as you type.
