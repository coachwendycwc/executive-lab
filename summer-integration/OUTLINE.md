## FILES (4)
1. **sponsor-vs-mentor-guide.html** — "Sponsor vs. Mentor Guide"
   - Named in Curriculum under both June (optional exact-match add) and Summer handouts.
   - Teaching frame (locked to Module 6 language): Mentors **advise you** (talk WITH you) · Sponsors **advocate for you** (talk ABOUT you, when you're not in the room). WOC reality: over-mentored, under-sponsored. (Scoped to Sponsor vs. Mentor only — Advocate/Connector card removed per Wendy 2026-08-01; the Mentor↔Sponsor↔Advocate three-way lives in Module 6's sorter.)
   - Sections: (1) The Distinction — two-card comparison (Mentor · Sponsor); (2) Who's actually on my side? — role audit table (Mentor / Sponsor / Not sure yet); (3) The advice-vs-advocacy gap — reflection; (4) Turn a mentor into a sponsor — the specific ask + script; (5) Commitment.

2. **energy-audit.html** — "Energy Audit" *(part of Energy Reset & the Cultural Tax Workbook)*
   - Frame: energy ≠ time. Map what drains vs. what restores across life/work domains.
   - Sections: (1) Energy across your domains — 1–5 rating grid (drained→energized), scored; (2) Drain inventory — table (drain · frequency · in my control? · one change); (3) Restore inventory — table (what restores · how long · when I'll do it next); (4) Reflection + one commitment.

3. **recovery-ritual-planner.html** — "Recovery Ritual Planner" *(part of the Workbook)*
   - Frame: recovery is designed, not stumbled into. Micro (daily) · Meso (weekly) · Macro (seasonal) rituals.
   - Sections: (1) Micro/Meso/Macro ritual tables (ritual · when/trigger · how long · how I protect it); (2) Boundaries that protect recovery — scripts; (3) My 3 non-negotiables; (4) Accountability commitment.

4. **cultural-tax-inventory.html** — "Cultural Tax Inventory" *(part of the Workbook)*
   - Frame (structural, not personal): the invisible, usually-uncompensated labor WOC carry — being "the only," affinity/DEI work, code-switching, educating others, emotional support, proving competence. The tax is imposed by systems; naming it is the first move to renegotiate it.
   - Sections: (1) Inventory — common cultural-tax categories, rate the toll 1–5, scored "tax load"; (2) Chosen vs. imposed — table (category · chosen/imposed · does it advance me? · keep/renegotiate/decline); (3) Renegotiation & recognition scripts; (4) Reflection: what I'll stop carrying uncompensated / ask to have recognized.
